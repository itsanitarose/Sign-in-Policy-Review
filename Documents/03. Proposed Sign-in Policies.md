# 03. Proposed Sign-in Policies

When a sign-in falls under two policies, the stricter requirement overrides the looser one

| Policy | Who | When | What we require | Exception |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. MFA for everyone | Staff and clinic users | Any app sign-in | Authenticator app or hardware key. Works the same at HQ, at home, and on the partner VPN. SMS does not count. | Shared clinic tablets. See 04. |
| 2. Sessions | Anyone signed in | A session is still open | Ends after 12 hours. Sign in again if the device or the place changes. | Service accounts that are not a person, such as svc-reports. |
| 3. Old protocols | User accounts | IMAP or any older sign-in | Blocked. These cannot ask for MFA. | The scanner and the old mail app only. Own account, fixed internal address. |
| 4. Admins | Admin accounts | Admin console, or any admin role | Company-managed device and a hardware key. | Break-glass accounts. See row 6. |
| 5. Vendors | Guest accounts, including BillingBridge | Any sign-in | Same MFA as staff. Their VPN does not skip it. Their device does not have to be ours. | None. |
| 6. Break-glass | The two emergency accounts | Any sign-in | Hardware key, and an alert every time. HQ does not skip the check. | None. Not for normal work. |
